# How to read this Storybook

Build in the order of the sidebar; each level is made only of the one before it.

1. **Tokens**: every colour, size, space, radius, motion and icon.
2. **Primitives**: the pieces drawn from tokens alone.
3. **Compositions**: primitives in a fixed configuration.
4. **Panels**: one tool, made of compositions.
5. **Layouts**: the window, its sidebar and its panes.

## A page

- **Overview**: what the piece is, its states as variants and their specifications. **Variants** with the **State** control shows one state at a time; the theme toolbar switches dark and light.
- Hover, focus, empty, loading, failure and armed are states of their piece, never pages of their own.
- **Guidelines**: only what a drawing cannot show, one rule per line.
- **Prompt for agents**: press **Copy prompt** and paste it with your task.
- **Not designed yet**: features the app has that still await a drawing, with their code location. The full list is `stories/GAPS.md`.

Token sources: `kanso/tokens.json` (names, values, both themes), `kanso/mods/kanso-panel/ui.js`, `kanso/themes/kanso.json` and `kanso/themes/kanso-day.json`.

## Prompt for agents

```text
Find the piece by its role in the five sections. Read its Overview, check its states in both themes, then copy its prompt. Compose larger surfaces from the existing tokens and primitives.
```
